Who we are

Dentistry,done quietly.

Precision Dental Clinic was built on one idea: good dentistry is quiet dentistry. Careful hands, honest advice, and a plan that respects your comfort and your budget. Every visit is unhurried, every instrument is sealed, and every price is written down before we begin.

  • HygieneSealed, autoclaved instruments
  • OpenEvery day, 10am to 10pm

Hygiene04 stages

Clean youcan see

  1. 01CleanedEvery instrument is scrubbed and washed by hand after each patient.
  2. 02SealedIt is then packed into a pouch that is sealed and dated.
  3. 03SterilisedThe sealed pouch goes through an autoclave: high-pressure steam that kills everything.
  4. 04Opened at your chairThe pouch is only opened in front of you, at the start of your visit.

Needles, gloves, bibs and suction tips are single-use: opened for you, used once, then thrown away.
