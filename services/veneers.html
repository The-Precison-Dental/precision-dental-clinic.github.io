Overview

Veneers are wafer-thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth. They change the colour, shape and line of a smile in a way whitening alone cannot, and they are planned with you before anything is touched.

Is it for you?

Usually a good fit if you have:

  • 01Chipped, worn or uneven front teeth
  • 02Stains that whitening cannot lift
  • 03Small gaps between front teeth
  • 04Teeth that look too short or mismatched

How it goes

  1. Step 01

    Plan the smile

    We talk through what you want to change, look at your bite and agree the shape and shade together.

  2. Step 02

    Prepare

    A very thin layer is polished from the front of each tooth and measured. Temporary veneers go on.

  3. Step 03

    Bond

    The finished veneers are tried in, checked with you in the mirror, then bonded in place.

Aftercare

  • 01Avoid biting nails, pens or hard objects with your front teeth.
  • 02Brush and floss as normal. Porcelain cleans just like enamel.
  • 03If you grind your teeth at night, ask us about a night guard.
